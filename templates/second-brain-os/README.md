# Second Brain OS

One Notion system for notes, projects, tasks, goals, finance, reading, hobbies and people, with a home dashboard and sample data.

**Status:** launching. The Notion Marketplace link will be added here when it is listed.

## Who it is for

Anyone who wants work and personal life in one place without stitching together separate templates for tasks, notes, money and habits.

## How it works

Everything you capture moves through four steps, and each dashboard is built for one of them.

| Capture | Organise | Act | Review |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Drop tasks, notes, contacts and expenses in as they happen. | File each item under an Area, Project or Topic. | Work from My Day, Tasks and Projects. | Close each week and month with a review. |

## What is inside

**Dashboards:** Home, My Day, My Week, My Year, Tasks, Projects, Knowledge, Goals & Habits, Finance, People & Mail, Hobbies & Health, Analytics, AI Prompts and Archive.

**29 linked databases**, grouped by what they cover:

| Area | Databases |
| --- | --- |
| Planning | Areas, Goals, Key Results, Projects, Project Updates, Tasks |
| Knowledge | Notes, Topics, Books, Highlights, Courses |
| People | People, Mail Log, Email Templates |
| Money | Accounts, Budgets, Transactions, Subscriptions, Savings Goals |
| Life | Habits, Journal, Reviews, Workouts, Hobbies, Hobby Sessions, Gear & Wishlist, Recipes, Meal Plan |
| Template | Changelog |

All databases live on one System page. The dashboards show the right slice of each, so you rarely need to open it.

**Sample data** fills every view so you can see how it works. Each database has a Sample data view, so it can be cleared in a few minutes.

## Requirements

Works on the free Notion plan. The one exception is the Analytics page, which uses four charts; the free plan allows one, so keep your favourite or upgrade to see all four. The AI Prompts page and AI Summary properties need Notion AI.

# Second Brain OS: setup and use

Set up Second Brain OS in ten minutes, learn how the parts fit together, then clear out the example data. The same guide is on the Start Here page inside the template.

## How the parts fit together

- **Areas** are the parts of life you keep up over time, such as Work, Health or Money. Projects, tasks, notes and goals all belong to an area.
- **Goals** are measured by **Key Results** and moved forward by **Projects**. Projects are broken into **Tasks** and reported on in **Project Updates**.
- **Notes** collect ideas, meetings and web clips. **Books**, **Highlights**, **Courses** and **Topics** turn them into a library you can search.
- **People** and the **Mail Log** form a personal CRM, with **Email Templates** for messages you send often.
- **Accounts**, **Budgets**, **Transactions**, **Subscriptions** and **Savings Goals** cover money.
- **Habits**, **Journal**, **Reviews**, **Workouts**, **Hobbies** and **Meal Plan** cover life outside work.

## 10-minute setup

1. Open **Home** and add it to your favourites from the ••• menu. It is your starting point every day. (1 min)
2. Open the **Areas** table on the Goals & Habits page and rename the areas to match your life. Keep 5 to 9. (2 min)
3. Add your real accounts and opening balances under **Accounts** on the Finance page, and set a monthly limit on each **Budget**. (2 min)
4. Rename the six habit columns in **Habits** to the habits you want to build. (1 min)
5. Add one active project and three tasks for it. Tick **My Day** on the task you will do first. (2 min)
6. Capture one note with the New button on the Knowledge page. (1 min)
7. Read the **AI Prompts** page and try the "Plan my day" prompt if you have Notion AI. (1 min)
8. Delete the sample data once you have seen how everything fits (below). (5 min, can wait)

## Daily and weekly rhythm

- **Every morning:** open My Day, tick My Day on the three to five tasks that matter, then work down the list.
- **During the day:** capture anything new into Tasks or Notes without sorting it.
- **Every week:** open My Week, process the Notes inbox, update active projects and write a weekly review.
- **Every month:** check Finance and Goals & Habits, then write a monthly review on My Year.

## Delete the sample data

Every database has a **Sample data** view that shows only the example rows. For each database, open it, choose the Sample data tab, select all rows (click the first row's handle, then press Ctrl+A or Cmd+A) and press Delete. The Start Here page inside the template has a checklist of all 29.

To keep some example Areas, Budgets or Email Templates as a starting point, untick **Sample** on those rows before you delete.

## Free and paid Notion plans

Everything works on the free plan except the Analytics page, which has four charts; the free plan allows one. Keep the chart you find most useful and delete the rest, or upgrade to see all four. The AI Prompts and AI Summary properties need Notion AI.
